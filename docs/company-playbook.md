# Growth Teams — Company Playbook
**Version 1.0 | Founded 2026 | growthteams.in**

---

## Vision

To build a globally recognised software development company from rural India — one that proves exceptional software can be built anywhere, and that creates high-skilled employment opportunities in underserved communities.

## Mission

We build custom software systems for operational businesses worldwide. We deliver on time, to spec, and to international standards — every time.

## Core Values

### 1. Delivery over promises
We say what we will do and we do what we said. Every engagement includes a fixed scope, fixed price, and fixed timeline. We do not ghost clients, reschedule milestones without cause, or make excuses. If something slips, we tell the client the same day.

### 2. Systems thinking
We build structure, not workarounds. Every system we build is designed to be maintained, extended, and operated without depending on us. We document everything, we build for maintainability, and we never create technical debt we cannot explain.

### 3. Long-term relationships
We measure success by how many clients come back for Phase 2. Transactional work is not our business model. We aim to be our clients' first call when they need to build something, not a vendor they found on Upwork.

### 4. The rural advantage
We are based in rural India. This gives us lower overheads without compromising quality. We pay our team well above local market rates. We do not compete on cheapness — we compete on quality and reliability.

### 5. No shortcuts in quality
We do not ship code we would be embarrassed to show a senior engineer. We write tests, we document APIs, we review each other's code. We treat every project as if we will need to maintain it for five years — because we might.

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

### Who We Serve Best

| Criterion | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Geography** | US, UK, Australia, UAE, Singapore |
| **Company size** | $1M – $50M revenue (SME to mid-market) |
| **Industry** | Operational businesses: logistics, retail, healthcare, education, real estate, field services |
| **Decision maker** | Founder, COO, or Operations Director |
| **Budget** | $15,000 – $80,000+ per project |
| **Pain point** | Manual processes, disconnected tools, no real-time visibility, growth causing chaos |
| **Values** | Wants a long-term partner, not cheapest quote. Thinks in systems, not patches. |

### Who We Do Not Serve Well

- Consumer app ideas without a business model ("Uber for X")
- Clients who want the cheapest option, not the best option
- Clients who cannot define what success looks like
- Projects below $10,000 (not enough scope for a production system)
- Clients who want a freelancer, not a company

## Service Offerings

### Core System ($15,000 – $40,000 | 30–45 days)
A complete, production-ready software system. Mobile app, web platform, or backend system built to exact requirements. Includes role-based access, core workflows, dashboards, deployment, and 30-day post-launch support.

### Enterprise System ($40,000 – $80,000 | 60–90 days)
Multi-module platforms with complex integrations, analytics layers, and AI features. Includes dedicated PM, advanced reporting, third-party integrations, and 90-day post-launch support.

### Ongoing Retainer ($3,000 – $8,000/month)
Ongoing development, maintenance, and feature additions. Available after a completed project. Minimum 3-month commitment.

## Sales Process

### Stage 1: Lead Qualification (Day 0–2)
- Lead source: LinkedIn, referral, website, cold outreach
- Qualification criteria: geography, budget, urgency, decision-maker access
- Action: Reply within 4 hours. Ask 5 qualifying questions via email/WhatsApp.
- Disqualify early if: budget under $10K, no clear problem, no timeline

### Stage 2: Discovery Call (Day 2–5)
- 30-minute video call
- Agenda: 5 min intro → 15 min understand their business → 5 min explain process → 5 min next steps
- Tool: Google Meet or Zoom
- Output: Discovery notes doc (internal)

### Stage 3: Proposal (Day 5–7)
- Fixed-price proposal document (use `proposal-template.md`)
- Delivered within 48 hours of discovery call
- Follow up once after 3 days if no response

### Stage 4: Contract & Onboarding
- Contract signed + 40% invoice raised
- Project setup begins on payment receipt
- Kickoff call within 3 business days

### Stage 5: Delivery
- Follow `sop/project-delivery.md`
- Client sees demo every 2 weeks
- All communication via agreed channel (Slack or email)

### Stage 6: Handover & Retention
- Final delivery, training, deployment
- 30-day support begins
- Retainer conversation at day 20 post-launch

## Quality Standards

### Definition of "Done"
A feature is done when:
1. It works as described in the scope document
2. It has been tested by a team member who did not build it
3. Edge cases and error states are handled
4. The code is reviewed and committed with a descriptive message
5. The client has seen a demo and approved it

### Code Standards
- All projects use Git with branch naming: `feature/`, `fix/`, `chore/`
- No direct commits to `main`
- Pull requests reviewed before merge
- README updated with setup and deployment instructions
- API documentation (Postman collection or Swagger) included

## Pricing Philosophy

We price on **business value**, not hours. A system that saves a client $200,000/year in manual labour should cost significantly more than one that saves $20,000/year — regardless of the number of hours to build it.

**Never compete on price.** If a client chooses a cheaper option, they were never our client.

**Floor pricing:** Never take a project below $10,000 USD. No exceptions.

## Reporting & Metrics (Monthly)

Track these every month:
- Revenue (billed vs collected)
- Active projects
- Proposals sent vs won (win rate)
- Client satisfaction (1 survey per project)
- Team utilisation (% of billable hours)
- Leads in pipeline

**Target win rate:** 30%+ of qualified proposals  
**Target utilisation:** 75%+ once team size reaches 3+
