# Growth Teams — International Service Agreement Template
**IMPORTANT: Have a local lawyer in your jurisdiction review before use**

---

```
SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made on [DATE]

Between:

GROWTH TEAMS, a software development studio operating from India
Email: hello@growthteams.in
("Service Provider")

And:

[CLIENT COMPANY NAME], a company incorporated under the laws of [COUNTRY/STATE]
Address: [CLIENT ADDRESS]
("Client")

Together referred to as the "Parties".
```

---

## 1. Services

### 1.1 Scope
Service Provider agrees to design, develop, and deliver the software system described in Proposal Reference **[GT-YEAR-NUMBER]**, attached to this Agreement as **Schedule A**.

### 1.2 Changes to Scope
Any changes to the agreed scope must be:
- Submitted by the Client in writing (email acceptable)
- Evaluated by Service Provider within 3 business days
- Agreed in writing before implementation begins
- Accompanied by an adjusted timeline and, if applicable, adjusted price

Verbal scope changes are not binding.

---

## 2. Delivery & Timeline

### 2.1 Estimated Timeline
Service Provider will make reasonable efforts to deliver the final system by **[DATE]** as outlined in Schedule A. This timeline is conditional on:
- Timely provision of required materials, credentials, and access from the Client
- Timely approvals and feedback from the Client (within 5 business days of demo)
- No material scope changes

### 2.2 Delays Caused by Client
If the Client causes delays (late feedback, withheld access, scope changes), the delivery timeline extends accordingly at no additional cost to the Client.

### 2.3 Delays Caused by Service Provider
If Service Provider anticipates a delay beyond 14 days, they will notify the Client in writing immediately with a revised timeline.

---

## 3. Fees & Payment

### 3.1 Total Fee
The total fixed fee for the Services described in Schedule A is **USD $[AMOUNT]**.

### 3.2 Payment Schedule

| Milestone | Amount | Trigger |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Initiation | $[AMOUNT] (40%) | On execution of this Agreement |
| Core Delivery | $[AMOUNT] (40%) | Client approval of core delivery demo |
| Final Launch | $[AMOUNT] (20%) | Deployment and final handover |

### 3.3 Payment Terms
Each invoice is due within **7 days** of issue. Late payments accrue interest at **1.5% per month** on the outstanding balance.

### 3.4 Currency
All amounts are in **United States Dollars (USD)** unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 3.5 Suspension for Non-Payment
Service Provider may pause all work if any payment is more than **14 days overdue**, after giving 48 hours written notice. Work resumes on payment of the outstanding amount.

---

## 4. Intellectual Property

### 4.1 Ownership on Full Payment
Upon receipt of all payments under this Agreement, Service Provider assigns to Client full ownership of:
- All custom source code written for this project
- All database schemas, API designs, and documentation created for this project
- All design assets created specifically for this project

### 4.2 Retained by Service Provider
Service Provider retains ownership of:
- Pre-existing tools, frameworks, libraries, and internal utilities used to build the project
- Any open-source components (subject to their respective licenses)
- General methodologies and architectural patterns

### 4.3 Third-Party Components
The final system may include open-source and third-party components. Service Provider will provide a list of such components and their licenses in the handover documentation.

### 4.4 IP Before Full Payment
Until all payments are received, all work product remains the property of Service Provider. Client may not use, copy, or deploy unpaid deliverables.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

### 5.1 Mutual Obligation
Each Party agrees to keep the other's **Confidential Information** strictly confidential. "Confidential Information" means any business, technical, financial, or operational information disclosed under this Agreement that is not publicly available.

### 5.2 Permitted Disclosures
Either Party may disclose Confidential Information:
- To employees or contractors who need it to perform under this Agreement
- As required by law or court order (with prior notice where possible)

### 5.3 Duration
This obligation continues for **3 years** after the termination of this Agreement.

### 5.4 Portfolio Use
Service Provider may reference the Client's name, industry, and a brief description of the project in its portfolio, proposals, and marketing materials, unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.

---

## 6. Warranties

### 6.1 Service Provider Warrants
- The Services will be performed with reasonable care and professional skill
- The deliverables will materially conform to the specifications in Schedule A
- The work is original and does not infringe any third-party IP (to the best of Service Provider's knowledge)
- The system will be free of material defects for **30 days** post-launch (the "Warranty Period")

### 6.2 Warranty Remedy
If a material defect is reported during the Warranty Period, Service Provider will fix it at no additional charge within **5 business days** of written notice.

### 6.3 No Warranty After Warranty Period
After the 30-day Warranty Period, ongoing support is available under a separate retainer agreement.

---

## 7. Limitation of Liability

### 7.1 Service Provider's liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid under this Agreement.

### 7.2 Neither Party is liable to the other for: loss of profits, loss of revenue, loss of data, indirect or consequential losses, even if advised of the possibility of such losses.

### 7.3 This limitation does not apply to: wilful misconduct, fraud, or death and personal injury caused by negligence.

---

## 8. Termination

### 8.1 Termination by Client
Client may terminate this Agreement with **14 days' written notice**. In this case:
- Work completed and approved is billable at the agreed milestone rates
- Work in progress at termination is billed at 50% of the next milestone rate
- Service Provider retains the initiation payment (40%)

### 8.2 Termination by Service Provider
Service Provider may terminate this Agreement if:
- Client fails to pay any invoice within 21 days of the due date
- Client materially breaches this Agreement and fails to remedy within 14 days of written notice

On termination by Service Provider for Client's breach, all amounts due are immediately payable.

---

## 9. Dispute Resolution

### 9.1 Good Faith
The Parties agree to first attempt to resolve any dispute through direct good-faith negotiation within **30 days** of written notice of the dispute.

### 9.2 Arbitration
If unresolved, disputes shall be referred to binding arbitration under the rules of [Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 / ICC / LCIA — choose based on client location].

### 9.3 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of **India**. (Note: If the Client is in a common law country, you may offer their jurisdiction as an alternative — discuss with a lawyer.)

---

## 10. General

### 10.1 Entire Agreement
This Agreement and Schedule A constitute the entire agreement between the Parties. It supersedes all prior proposals, discussions, and agreements.

### 10.2 Amendments
Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

### 10.3 Assignment
Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent.

### 10.4 Severability
If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

### 10.5 Independent Contractor
Service Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship.
