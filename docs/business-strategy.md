**Internal Document | Founder Reference**

## The Thesis

Most Indian software agencies compete on price. This creates a race to the bottom that produces poor work, burned-out teams, and no sustainable business.

Growth Teams competes on **reliability and systems thinking** for operational businesses in high-purchasing-power markets (US, UK, AU, UAE). We charge premium rates, deliver to spec, and build long-term relationships. This creates a fundamentally different business: fewer clients, higher margins, lower stress, and compounding reputation.

## 10-Year Growth Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation (2026–2027)
**Goal:** Prove the model. First 3 international clients. $50K–$80K revenue.

**Focus:**
- Get the website live and credible (growthteams.in)
- Build 3 real case studies (POS SAAS, FarmToHome, Fleet360)

- Land 2–3 clients from diaspora network or direct cold outreach

**Revenue targets:**

**Key risks:**
- No incoming leads (mitigation: consistent LinkedIn content, cold email)
- Underpricing out of fear (mitigation: commit to floor pricing in the playbook)
- Delivering solo without systems (mitigation: document everything from Day 1)

### Phase 3: Software Company (2029–2031)
**Goal:** Team of 10–15. $750K–$1.2M revenue. First SaaS product.

**Triggers:**
- 5+ concurrent projects manageable without founder in every meeting
- Documented systems for sales, delivery, and support

**Focus:**

- Submit for awards (Deloitte Fast 50, etc.)
- Begin speaking at small conferences / podcasts

**Revenue model shifts:**
- Year 4: 80% services / 20% SaaS
- Year 5: 60% services / 40% SaaS

### Phase 4: Scale (2031–2036)
**Goal:** 50–100 employees. $3M–$10M revenue. Recognised brand.

- SaaS revenue exceeds services revenue

## Client Acquisition Channels

### Priority 1: LinkedIn (Start Now, High ROI)
**Why:** B2B decision-makers are on LinkedIn. Founders, COOs, Operations Directors.

**Strategy:**
- Connect with 20 ICP contacts/day (with personalised note, no pitch)
- Post 3× per week: case study breakdowns, operational software insights, rural India angle
- DM follows after 2 weeks of engagement, not cold pitch
- Target: 500 targeted connections in 90 days

### Priority 2: Referrals (From Day 1)
After every project, explicitly ask:
> "Is there anyone in your network who might benefit from a similar system? We're happy for you to make an introduction."

One referral per year compounds faster than any marketing channel.

### Priority 3: Cold Email (Month 3+)
**Target:** Operations Directors, COOs, founders of operational businesses (logistics, retail, healthcare) in US/AU.

**Process:**
- Find contacts on Apollo.io or LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Research company pain points (Google, LinkedIn posts, Glassdoor)
- Send 3-email sequence: personalised value observation → case study → soft ask
- Volume: 30–50 personalised emails/week
- Target: 5–10% reply rate, 1–2% conversion to call

### Priority 4: Content (Month 6+)
- Case study posts: "How we built [X] in 45 days"
- Process posts: "Why most operational software fails"
- Contrarian posts: "Why we don't compete on price"
- YouTube shorts or Reels explaining operational software concepts

### Priority 5: Directories (Year 2+)
- Clutch.co (get 3+ reviews)
- Contra.com
- Toptal (after 2 years, apply for agency partner program)
- GoodFirms

## Niche Selection Rationale

### Why operational businesses?
1. **High pain:** Manual processes cost operational companies real money. The ROI of a system is calculable.
2. **High budgets:** These companies have revenue. They can pay $15K–$80K.
3. **Repeatable:** A fleet management system is 70% similar across clients. We build faster over time.
4. **Your portfolio:** POS SAAS, FarmToHome, Fleet360 — you already have proof.

### Why not:
- **Consumer apps:** Too speculative. Clients often have no revenue yet.
- **E-commerce:** Dominated by Shopify/WooCommerce agencies. Hard to differentiate.
- **Social/media apps:** High complexity, low ROI for the builder.
- **SaaS for SaaS:** Requires deep product knowledge. Do this after Phase 2.

## Using AI to Stay Lean

AI tools dramatically reduce hours needed per project. Use them to maintain quality while keeping team small:

| Task | Tool | Time Saved |
|------|------|-----------|
| Boilerplate code | GitHub Copilot | 30–40% |
| API documentation | Mintlify / Stoplight | 60% |
| Test generation | Copilot + pytest | 40% |

| Client proposals | Claude / GPT-4 (draft) | 50% |
| Meeting notes | Otter.ai | 80% |
| Internal docs | Notion AI | 40% |

**Rule:** AI assists humans. Every AI output is reviewed by a developer. We never ship AI-generated code without understanding it.

## Legal & Compliance (India)

### Business Structure
- **Start as:** Individual / Proprietorship (immediate, no cost)
- **Year 1:** Register as LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) — ~₹5,000–₹10,000
- **Year 3:** Convert to Private Limited Company — required for investment, hiring at scale

### Tax
- Register for GST once turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh (or if serving international clients, register for export benefits)
- International payments are not subject to GST (zero-rated export of services)
- Open a **Current Account** at a bank that supports SWIFT/foreign remittances (HDFC, ICICI, Axis)
- Consider Wise Business for receiving USD (lower fees than SWIFT)

### Compliance (Year 1)
- File income tax returns annually
- Maintain invoices for all international receipts
- FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act): Receive payments within 9 months of service delivery

### Contracts
- Use the contract template in `docs/legal/contract-template.md`
- Have it reviewed by a lawyer before first use (~₹5,000–₹15,000 one-time)
- Keep signed copies of all agreements for 7 years
