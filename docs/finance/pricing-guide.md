## Fixed Price Calculation

### Formula
```
Project Price = (Estimated Hours × Blended Rate) × Risk Multiplier × Value Multiplier
```

- **Blended Rate:** $65–$80/hr (accounts for mixed senior/junior team)
- **Risk Multiplier:** 1.0 (well-defined) → 1.4 (ambiguous or complex integrations)
- **Value Multiplier:** 1.0 (standard) → 1.5 (high-ROI system, mission-critical)

### Hour estimation by project type

| Project Type | Estimated Hours | Base Price |
|--------------|----------------|------------|
| Simple mobile app (1 role, CRUD) | 200–300 hrs | $15K–$22K |
| Business web platform (3 roles) | 300–450 hrs | $22K–$35K |
| Full CRM / booking system | 400–600 hrs | $30K–$45K |
| POS / multi-branch system | 500–700 hrs | $38K–$55K |
| Enterprise multi-module platform | 700–1000 hrs | $55K–$80K |
| AI integration (add-on) | 100–200 hrs | $8K–$15K |

### Always add a buffer
Add 20% to your raw estimate. Software projects always have unknowns. The buffer is not dishonest — it is professional risk management.

## Payment Structure

### Standard (all projects)
- **40%** at project initiation (before any work starts)
- **40%** at core delivery milestone (mid-project demo approval)
- **20%** at final launch and handover

### Why this structure
- 40% upfront: filters serious clients, covers initial team costs
- 40% at milestone: aligned to value delivered, keeps project moving
- 20% at launch: client has incentive to complete feedback and accept handover

### Invoicing
- All invoices in USD (for international clients)
- Payment terms: Net 7 (due within 7 days of invoice)
- Accepted methods: Bank wire (SWIFT), Wise, PayPal Business
- Include GSTIN if Indian client (GST applies at 18%)

## Retainer Pricing

### When to propose a retainer
Propose a retainer at Day 20 post-launch. Client has seen the system working and trust is established.

### Pricing
| Level | Hours/Month | Price/Month |
|-------|------------|------------|
| Maintenance | 20 hrs | $1,800 |
| Growth | 40 hrs | $3,200 |
| Partner | 80 hrs | $5,800 |

### Retainer rules
- Minimum 3-month commitment
- Unused hours do not roll over (incentivises regular use)
- Client gets priority response (within 2 hours vs standard 4)
- Monthly sync call included

## Negotiation Scripts

### "Your price is too high"
> "I understand price is a factor. Can I ask — what is the business impact of not solving this problem for the next 6 months? Our price is fixed and includes delivery risk. If you hire cheaper and the project fails, you pay twice. What we're offering is a guaranteed outcome."

### "Can you do it for X (below floor)?"
> "That number doesn't allow us to do this properly. If we cut corners to hit that price, you'll spend more fixing it later. What I can do is reduce the scope to fit the budget — let's talk about what the must-have version looks like."

### "We need it in 2 weeks"
> "That's not possible for a system of this scope, and any agency that promises that is not being honest with you. We can do a phased delivery — a working core in 30 days and enhancements in the following 30. Would that work?"

### "Can we pay milestone-by-milestone?"
> "Our standard is 40-40-20. We need the 40% upfront to allocate the team and begin work. This also protects you — you know we have skin in the game. We don't start work without it."

## Red Flags (Walk Away)

Walk away from a potential client who:
1. Asks for a discount before seeing the proposal
2. Cannot clearly describe what success looks like
3. Has worked with 3+ agencies and had bad experiences with all of them
4. Wants to pay 100% on completion
5. Refuses to sign a contract
6. Asks you to "just build something quick and we'll figure out the details later"
7. Disrespects your time (no-shows discovery call with no notice)
